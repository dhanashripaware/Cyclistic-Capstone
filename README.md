# Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis Case Study

## Project Overview
This capstone project analyzes 12 months of historical trip data (October 2025 – September 2026) for Cyclistic, a fictional bike-share company in Chicago. The objective is to understand how annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently to help guide a new marketing strategy designed to convert casual riders into annual members.

## Data Source & Tools
- **Dataset:** 12 months of historical trip data cleaned and processed.
- **Data Cleaning & Analysis:** Google BigQuery (`cyclistic_analysis.sql`)
- **Data Visualization:** Tableau Public Dashboard

## Key Insights
- Annual members use bikes most frequently during weekday morning and evening commute hours.
- Casual riders peak heavily on weekends and take longer trips on average, indicating leisure usage.
